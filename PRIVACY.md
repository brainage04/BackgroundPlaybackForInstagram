# Privacy Policy

Background Playback for Instagram does not collect, store, sell, or transmit user data.

It runs three scripts on `www.instagram.com` pages, all inside your browser:

- One tells Instagram's own code the page is always visible and focused, so Instagram does
  not pause videos when you switch tabs or windows.
- One notes which media buffers belong to each video, so that after you pick a new video
  quality the extension can clear the old quality from the buffer.
- One shows player controls over the video under your pointer, unmutes videos once you have
  clicked on the page, and passes your play, pause, seek, volume, quality,
  picture-in-picture and full-screen choices to that video or Instagram's own player.

To place the controls, these scripts look at the page's layout and Instagram's player
state. Nothing they see leaves the page.

The extension does not:

- collect personal information or browsing history
- send page content anywhere
- store settings or user data
- use analytics
- contact external servers
- execute remotely hosted code
